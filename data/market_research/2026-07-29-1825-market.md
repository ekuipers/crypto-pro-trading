### Strategy fit — mismatched in a specific, quantifiable way

The 6-point score is 4 parts trend/momentum (EMA cross, MACD, 4H regime, and volume-as-confirmation)
and 2 parts mean-reversion (RSI oversold, BB %b near lower band). In the current low-ATR chop:

- The **trend components are all firing bearish simultaneously** (8× 4H death, 8× 15m death), which
  drives scores to −1.5…−3.0 and makes the TA-sell threshold (≤ −2) the active rule rather than any
  entry rule. Four symbols are at or past it.
- The **mean-reversion components cannot rescue a score** here: RSI 32–45 is mostly in the dead band
  (neither < 30 nor rising within 40–65), so the two counter-trend points are unavailable exactly
  when the trend points are maximally negative. The score is behaving as a one-sided trend filter.
- That is the *intended* design (CLAUDE.md: "only trade with the 4H + daily trend"), so this is fit,
  not mismatch, in the narrow sense — the system correctly refuses to trade. The mismatch is
  economic, below.

  the documented 2-cycle widening is clamped away (see the project-verification report, finding A).
- **FAIL** — Profitability: PF 0.30, payoff 0.57, −$7,043 over 331 round trips, break-even win rate

## Recommendations

Advice only — no orders, no code or config changes.

1. **Stay flat.** No watchlist symbol is within 2.0 points of the 2.5 half-size gate, 8 of 10 are in
   a 4H death cross, and the live net R:R gate blocks 9 of 10 on cost alone. Nothing to do.
2. **Treat the 15-min timeframe as the primary suspect**, not the score. Move execution to 1H/4H
   where the ATR (1.02–2.22% on 4H) can pay a 0.6–1.1% round trip, or cut the watchlist to symbols
   whose spread is under ~0.15% — today that is BTC and ETH only.
3. **Reconcile sizing with the exit stop**: size on `swingLowStopPrice` distance rather than
   1.5×ATR(15m), or the 1%-risk rule remains nominal.
4. **Do not raise position count or loosen gates** while payoff is 0.57. With expectancy at
   −2.27%/trade net, every additional trade is negative EV; the throttle being active is the single
   most protective thing currently in force.
5. **Fix the stop-escalation clamp before adding any position** in AVAX or LTC — their stops are not
   currently executable at the configured band.
6. **Rebuild a walk-forward harness (Node) before the next strategy change.** Three consecutive
   scoring commits now rest on single live scans, against a live record that shows no edge.
7. Watch for a 4H golden-cross flip on ETH and AAVE (the only two already golden) as the earliest
   credible regime turn; require the daily SMA20/50 to confirm before treating it as mark-up.
