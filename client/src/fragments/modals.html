Terms of Service

        
      

      

        
CryptoPro Trader is an educational, autonomous paper-crypto spot trading cockpit. It ships in paper spot trading mode by default and connects to Alpaca's paper trading API, which simulates order execution with no real funds at risk.

        
No live trading. This application never places an order on a live brokerage account. Setting the trading mode to "Live" makes it read-only: it may display balances, positions, orders and prices from your own live Alpaca keys for insight, but order placement and portfolio management are available in paper mode only.
